💌 Theme FAQ

Does this require JavaScript?

Nope! The tabs on the right side and the color changer on the left rely entirely on the "CSS Radio Button Hack" tying label elements to hidden radio inputs. 🐰✨

How do I edit the avatar?

You can change the image by finding the `.avatar` class in the CSS and swapping out the Pinterest URL for your own direct image link! (🎮^▽^)
